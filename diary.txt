W16AAPPLE-21 review fixes: a relay-safe duplicate key, a cursor that accepts any

W16AAPPLE-22: Handle the CONNECTED status and server_newer.
Read the status (new, connected, running) and server_newer from the CONNECTED
frame and carried them into the connection state. When the status is running,
the composer is held and failed turns are revived. When it is connected, cards a
finished turn left spinning are settled. When the Host is idle, a failed turn
stays failed. Messages typed while offline are marked sent once the connection
returns, and the server_newer merge is tested. Documented how each status is
handled.
